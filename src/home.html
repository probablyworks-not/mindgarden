mindgarden

This is my mind, laid out like a garden —
some rows I planted on purpose,
some grew when I wasn't looking.

There are weeds here too, quick and uninvited,
and things I'm still learning to name.

I am the gardener here.
I pull what doesn't serve, I water what might,
I kneel some mornings not knowing what will come of it.

Nothing is finished. Nothing is wasted.
Everything here is still becoming.
